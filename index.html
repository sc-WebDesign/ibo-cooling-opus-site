Three things the listing never mentions

Repair is only part of it

These are the three subjects IBO Cooling keeps as saved highlights on Instagram. They are the difference between an engineer who swaps parts and one who looks after plant.

Condenser cleaning

A blocked condenser makes a healthy unit work like a failing one, then fail like one. Cleaning is the cheapest job on this page and it prevents the most expensive.

Commissioning

New equipment set up and commissioned properly, rather than plugged in and hoped for. This is installation work, not just breakdown work.

R290

The natural refrigerant that new commercial equipment is moving to. It has its own handling requirements, and this is an engineer who keeps a highlight reel of the work.
