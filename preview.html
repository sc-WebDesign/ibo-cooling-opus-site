What comes through the door

The equipment a kitchen or a shop runs on

Every machine listed here appears in a customer review or in the job photographs on the IBO Cooling Instagram feed. If yours is not on the list, ring and ask.

Cold rooms

Walk-in rooms running warm, iced up or failing to hold temperature. A room that has been climbing for days can be back down to 2 degrees within half an hour of the repair finishing.

Display cabinets and chillers

Glass fronted units full of stock, the ones customers see. Including an eight year old ice cream display cabinet that two other repairers had already given up on.

Ice machines

Ice machine repair is a job in its own right here. Bars and takeaways stop serving without one.

Soft serve and ice cream machines

A broken soft serve machine can shut a counter down, and one specialises in refrigerators but knew a lot about ice cream machines too. Those get looked at the same day where the day allows it.

Under counter and prep fridges

The units built into a service line. A single door under counter fridge can have a slow leak found inside the hour.

Freezers, seals and doors

Perished door seals let cold out and money with it. One job covered a fridge freezer and two more fridges, with the seals sourced as part of the work.

The part nobody advertises

The calls other firms turn down

Commercial refrigeration is not domestic fridge repair with bigger boxes. It is different equipment, different refrigerants and different consequences when it fails. Plenty of firms advertise it and then decline the call when it comes, which is how businesses end up ringing round for half a day with a cold room climbing.

People end up hunting for somebody who will actually attend. They should not have to.

Three things the listing never mentions

Repair is only part of it

These three are the difference between an engineer who swaps parts and one who looks after plant. None of them appears anywhere on the Google listing.

Condenser cleaning

A blocked condenser makes a healthy unit work like a failing one, then fail like one. Cleaning is the cheapest job on this page and it prevents the most expensive.

Commissioning

New equipment set up and commissioned properly, rather than plugged in and hoped for. This is installation work, not just breakdown work.

R290

The natural refrigerant that new commercial equipment is moving to. It has its own handling requirements, and this is an engineer who keeps a highlight reel of the work.

Planned

Booked at a time that suits the kitchen

Unplanned

Happens on the worst day of the year

  • A heat wave, a full room and a rising number
  • Phoning down a list of firms who say they do not do commercial
  • Waiting on a part nobody stocks
  • Service carrying on around an engineer
  • Stock is the first thing you count

Some businesses have their fridges and cold rooms looked after for years rather than calling in a crisis. That is the arrangement worth asking about.
