## Two drawings out, one photograph in

The hero carried a **display chiller drawn in CSS** with a 2.0 degree readout on
its face, and a second section carried a **drawn condenser** with fan blades and
fins. Both have gone. The condenser was removed with nothing in its place, which
is deliberate.

## Why a site this long has one photograph

**The stock for this trade is nearly all the wrong trade.** Four searches
produced two categories and almost nothing else:

- **Retail beverage coolers packed with branded drinks.** Coca-Cola fridges,
  Ben & Jerry's freezers, supermarket dairy aisles. Those are shops, not the
  engineer who repairs the shop's equipment, and every one of them puts somebody
  else's brand on the page.
- **Domestic and commercial air conditioning.** Condensers on apartment
  balconies, split units on facades, technicians on rooftops. A different trade,
  and the page would be claiming it silently.

Rejected along the way: a "condenser coil" that turned out to be **a copper CPU
heatsink on a studio backdrop**, an air conditioner with **Ballu** on the
casing, a New Delhi workshop, and a set of walk-in wardrobes that the search
returned for "walk-in cold storage".

## The one that works, and what was cropped out of it

**`gauges.jpg`** is a **refrigerant manifold gauge set** in an engineer's hands,
red high side and blue low side. It is the instrument nobody outside this trade
owns, it is identical in every country, and it is what you actually read to find
a slow leak or get a cold room back down to temperature.

Two things were cropped out: **the engineer's glasses and temple**, because a
readable face on a spec site reads as the owner, and **a charging hose printed
`R410a`**, because that is an air conditioning refrigerant and this page is
about R290.
